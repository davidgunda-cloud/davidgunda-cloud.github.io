Forage Plant Guide

Trees, shrubs, grasses and herbs recorded during the fieldwork in northern Tanzania and in Valkó Forest, Hungary. Scientific names have been checked against current botanical names. Photos will be added.

Do you know a Kiswahili or local name for any of these plants? Please share it with us at contact@davigherbivores.org, and tell us the plant and the language or area the name comes from.
